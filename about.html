Our Mission:

To promote equity and combat food inscurity at RCHS to create a better learning enviornment.

FFT Beginnings

According to 2020-2021 statistics from the Virginia Department of Education, ⅓ of students in Rockbridge County High School are eligible to receive free or reduced-cost lunch, meaning that ⅓ of RCHS students are at high-risk of becoming food insecure. That's where Food for Thought steps in. We aim to help Wildcat students and their families by providing free food, clothing, and hygiene products. We believe that our efforts make RCHS a better learning environment by providing everyone with the materials they need to succeed.

Food for Thought was started by students Gianna McKeeman, Emily McClung, and Caroline Diette under the mentorship of Dr. Sandra Hayslette in an attempt to combat food insecurity at RCHS. We have been serving the Rockbridge County High School community since Fall of 2022 and continue to work to create a sustainable food and necessities pantry that will exist to help the community for years to come.
