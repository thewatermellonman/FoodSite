FAQ

Is this fully anonymous?

Yes! We never collect your name or address. The only information we need is size and ages of you household.


Are your services free to use?

Yes! All services are 100% free


How does your food pantry differ from other food pantrise?

We operate differently in two main ways. First, we emphasize anonymity and never collect names or addresses. Second, we emphasize putting together meals that are filling, nutritious, and appetizing. Food for Thought takes pride in curating cohesive, delicious meals.


Who runs Food for Thought?

Food for Thought is fully student-led. The pantry is managed by a team of 9 student managers under the mentorship of a local teacher.
